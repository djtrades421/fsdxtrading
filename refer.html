How it works

Four steps. Whop handles the tracking and the money.

Hit the button above. It opens your FSD-X affiliate page on Whop with your link already generated — copy it and you're done. Log in first if it asks.

STEP 02

Share it

Your prop firm group chat, a YouTube description, a Discord you're already in, the trader friend who keeps asking what you're running.

STEP 03

They join

Whop tags the signup to your link automatically. Nothing for them to type, nothing for you to claim.

STEP 04

You get paid

Commission posts to your Whop balance after a 30-day holding period, then repeats each cycle they renew.

Heads up: the rate column will look blank

In your Whop dashboard, the Affiliate rate column shows the public rate — and we've deliberately switched the public rate off, so that only members earn on referrals. It will show blank or a dash. Blank does not mean zero. You're on 30%, and your commission will appear in your balance normally.

Free trials count — on day 31

Someone you refer starts on the free trial like everyone else. Your commission begins when their first real payment goes through, not when they sign up.

30% is the starting rate

It isn't a ceiling.

If you're steadily bringing in members who stick around, we'll raise your rate — up to 50%. We come to you. You don't have to apply, hit a target, or climb anything.

There's no tier chart here on purpose. We're not going to publish a number and have people chase it — that's how you end up with twenty signups who all cancel in month two. We look at who's actually helping, and we adjust. If you think you're one of them and we haven't noticed, just tell us.

The rules

Short list. Break one and the link gets shut off.

✓
Say you're an affiliate

If you post the link publicly, disclose that you earn a commission. Required by law, not just by us.

✓
Use our numbers, not invented ones

Everything on the Live Track Record and Results pages is fair game. Don't promise income, guaranteed wins, or a funded account.

✗
No paid ads on our name

Don't bid on "FSD-X", "Knightfall" or "ORB PRO" in Google or Meta ads. You'd be buying traffic that was already coming to us.

✗
No self-referrals

Your own account, an alt, a family member on your card. Whop flags it and the commission gets reversed.

✗
No spam

Cold DMs, comment dumps, or posting the link in servers that didn't ask for it. It reflects on us, not just you.

✗
Don't resell or repackage

This is for sending people to us. It isn't a licence to bundle, rebrand, or sell access to the scripts.
