Section 01

What the Copy Trader Is

The FSD-X Copy Trader ("the Copy Trader") is web software. It connects to your Tradovate logins through Tradovate's official sign-in (OAuth). You choose one of your own accounts as the leader. When the leader's position changes, the Copy Trader sends orders to the follower accounts you choose so their positions match, using the size rules you set.

  • It only copies between accounts you own or control. It never copies FSD-X, another member, or any signal provider.
  • It is not a signal service, not a managed account, and not financial advice. FSD-X does not decide what, when, or how much you trade.
  • It runs on FSD-X servers. Nothing is installed in NinjaTrader or on your computer.
  • A Copy Trader plan also includes the FSD-X member platform and Nexus 2.0. It does not include the FSD-X indicator suite, the Auto-Trader, Scout Alerts, the live trade room, or the VIP Discord channels.

Section 04

What Is and Isn't Copied

  • Followers copy the leader's net position only. The leader's working stop-loss and take-profit orders are not copied to followers.
  • Followers exit when the leader's position closes. If the Copy Trader is stopped, disconnected, or down for any reason, follower accounts have no stop of their own and can stay open.
  • Risk tools (daily loss, daily profit lock, per-trade loss, max contracts, trading windows, news filters, profit-target lock) are checked on a timer, not tick by tick. Fast markets, gaps, and latency mean a limit can be exceeded before it acts. They are aids, not guarantees.
  • Disarming the Copy Trader stops copying. It does not close open positions.
