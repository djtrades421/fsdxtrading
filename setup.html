Getting Started

New here? This walks the whole thing start to finish — picking a plan, claiming your access, and getting the indicators onto your chart. Under 10 minutes end to end.

Step 1 — Choose a Plan Step 2 — Checkout Step 3 — Site Account Step 4 — Indicators Step 5 — Discord Step 6 — Add to Chart Step 7 — Nexus Step 8 — Secure Account Step 9 — You're Live
1

Choose Your Membership

Monthly, quarterly or yearly — all the same access

One membership covers the whole suite: the indicators, the Auto-Trader, the live trade room and the member site. The only difference between the plans is how often you are billed.

  1. 01.Open the membership page and pick monthly, quarterly or yearly
  2. 02.Read the terms when they come up and accept them
  3. 03.You are sent to Whop to check out
Every plan starts on a free trial You are not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel from Whop at any point before then.
2

Check Out on Whop

This is where you give your TradingView username

Checkout is handled by Whop. One field on this screen matters more than the rest.

  1. 01.Enter your TradingView username when asked — this is what the indicators get granted to
  2. 02.Use the username shown on your TradingView profile, not your email address
  3. 03.Finish checkout — you are sent straight back to fsdxtrading.com
Get this one right The username is case sensitive and it is how your invite-only access is issued. If you mistype it, message us in Discord or at fsdxteam@gmail.com and we will correct it.
3

Create Your Site Account

One button. No licence key, no form to fill in.

Your site account gives you the member area — dashboard, journal, playbook, backtest sessions and Scout alerts. Whop already knows who you are, so there is nothing to type.

  1. 01.Checkout drops you on fsdxtrading.com/welcome automatically
  2. 02.Click Continue with Whop and approve when Whop asks
  3. 03.That's it — your account is created, your membership is attached, and you land on your dashboard
No key needed here Your licence key is read straight from Whop. You only need to handle it yourself for Nexus in Step 7.
Saw an error the first time? If Whop made you log in partway through you may see "that sign-in attempt expired". The site retries this for you automatically now — if it ever sticks, click Continue with Whop once more.

4

Claim Your TradingView Indicators

Invite-only scripts have to be claimed before they appear

The indicators are invite-only, so they need claiming on Whop before TradingView will show them to you.

  1. 01.Open your Whop membership
  2. 02.Click Claim next to the TradingView option
  3. 03.Confirm your TradingView username — the same one from checkout
  4. 04.Give it a few minutes to come through
Not showing up? It can take up to five minutes. If it still has not appeared, the username is usually the culprit — check it for typos and capitalisation.
Whop dashboard → Claim TradingView indicators
5

Claim Your Discord Access

The live room, VIP channels and Scout alerts

Discord access is claimed the same way, on the same membership page.

  1. 01.Back in your Whop membership, click Claim next to the Discord option
  2. 02.Authorize the connection when Discord prompts you
  3. 03.You are added to the private channels automatically
Already in the server? Make sure you claim with the same Discord account you actually use. If your roles do not appear, open a ticket in the server.
Whop dashboard → Claim Discord access
6

Add the Indicators to Your Chart

Claiming grants access — you still have to pull them onto a chart

Once claimed, the scripts sit in your TradingView library until you add them. This is the step people miss.

  1. 01.Open a chart on TradingView and click Indicators in the top toolbar
  2. 02.Go to Invite-only scripts in the left-hand list
  3. 03.Click FSD-X ORB PRO to add it to the chart
Nothing in the list? Invite-only scripts only appear once the claim has gone through, and it can lag by a few minutes. Refresh TradingView and check again before assuming something is wrong.
7

Activate Nexus 2.0

The one place your member key is still typed by hand

Nexus 2.0 is a Chrome extension that runs as a side panel inside TradingView.

  1. 01.In your Whop membership, open the Software tab and copy your licence key
  2. 02.Install FSD-X Nexus 2.0 from the Chrome Web Store →
  3. 03.Open TradingView and click the Nexus icon in your Chrome toolbar
  4. 04.Click KEY top right, paste the key and click Activate
Key locks to your device Your key activates on the first device you use it on. To move it to a new device, ask in Discord or email fsdxteam@gmail.com.
Whop dashboard → Software → Copy your key
8

Secure Your Account

Password, then lock it down — about a minute

Continue with Whop is there to get you set up, not to be your login forever. Give yourself a password and that becomes your everyday way in.

  1. 01.Open your profile and use Set a Password
  2. 02.Optional — turn Sign in with Whop off, so email and password is the only way into your account
  3. 03.Turn on two-factor with an authenticator app
Why that order Two-factor protects email sign-in. While Continue with Whop is still switched on, your Whop account security governs that door — so either switch Whop sign-in off here, or turn on 2FA at Whop as well.
