// Quick Answers

Quick answers.

How do I get it?+

It comes with the FSD-X Trading Pro — NinjaTrader 8 membership, along with Volume, Raven, the member platform, Nexus, the live trade room and the VIP Discord. Pay once, or subscribe. See pricing.

Which email does my license use?+

The email you log into NinjaTrader with. You enter it at checkout. If it's different from your Whop email, that's fine — just make sure it matches your NinjaTrader login. You can change it later from the NinjaTrader hub.

Do I need TradingView or a bridge service for it?+

No. It runs inside NinjaTrader 8 and places orders through the account you connect there. No webhooks, alerts or third-party automation service.

Can I use it on prop firm accounts?+

If your firm lets you connect through NinjaTrader and allows automated trading. Rules vary by firm — check yours before running it hands-free. Manual mode is always an option.

Is the TradingView version still available?+

Yes. Knightfall is on TradingView too, with Auto Trade built in. It's its own membership, FSD-X Trading Pro — TradingView, with the same payment options. See TradingView pricing.
