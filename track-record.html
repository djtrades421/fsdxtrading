// Important — Please Read

Each variant tab shows the signal output of one configuration of FSD-X ORB Pro on MNQ — every setup it generated over the period shown, wins and losses, with no trades removed. Results are shown as signaled, at the position size the strategy itself assigns using the $400 risk-per-trade profile, net of an assumed $1.04 round-turn commission per contract. Variants run at the same time and carry different risk. Their numbers are never combined.

These are not the results of any individual trading account, and they do not represent the returns of any member or of FSD-X Trading. Actual results will differ based on the variant and risk profile selected, execution, slippage, fees, and whether a trader takes every signal. The sample shown is small and is not a statistically reliable basis for projecting future performance.

Each trade is shown unmanaged — closed only by the exits listed for its variant, with no breakevens, partial exits or discretionary exits — which is how the system signals it, not how any individual trader manages it. When a variant is updated, its record keeps running and the version in use is shown at the top of its tab. Variant 1 includes trades from December 16, 2025 to April 30, 2026 signaled by ORB Pro v5, an indicator version, marked v5; they come from that version's own trade list and were checked against the trades called in the live room at the time. The list shows the most recent trades; the stats above it cover every trade in the variant's record.

The seven-year backtest on our research data page is simulated over historical data and is not blended with this record.
