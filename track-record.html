// Important — Please Read

This page shows the signal output of the FSD-X ORB PRO strategy, Knightfall version 2.0, on MNQ — every setup it generated over the period shown, wins and losses, with no trades removed. Results are shown as signaled, at the position size the strategy itself assigns using the $400 risk-per-trade profile (which produces 1–3 MNQ contracts depending on grade and stop distance), net of an assumed $2.50 round-turn commission per contract.

These are not the results of any individual trading account, and they do not represent the returns of any member or of FSD-X Trading. Actual results will differ based on the risk profile selected, execution, slippage, fees, and whether a trader takes every signal. A different risk setting produces different contract counts, and therefore different dollar results, from the same signals. The sample shown here is small and is not a statistically reliable basis for projecting future performance.

Earlier versions of ORB PRO are not represented here. The seven-year backtest shown on our research data page is simulated and covers the strategy logic over historical data; this page covers only what version 2.0 has signaled since the beta went live on May 1, 2026.
