// How It Works
Three steps. Then you walk away.
01
The strategy fires

A graded breakout triggers when the bar closes — same A+ to F scoring, taking A+ through C, and it sizes the position to the grade, just like Knightfall MK1.

02
Your service receives it

It sends the order — direction, contracts, entry, stop, and targets — to the automation service you connect (PickMyTrade, TradersPost, and others).

03
Your broker executes

Your stop and targets sit at your broker and manage the trade for you. At the end of the session it closes out on its own — and skips holidays and half-days automatically.

The strategy emits alerts only — execution happens through a third-party service and broker you configure and control.
