01The engine

Every trade is measured in R: its result divided by that trade's own stop. That keeps these numbers the same at any risk setting. Risk and Flag Sizing change position size, not which trades fire or how they end.

Profit factor, every year

By grade

Profit factor in R. Grade sets size: A+ to B+ full, B 75%, C+ and C 50%. D and F aren't traded.

By warning flags on the breakout

Stop past a prior day/week high or low · overnight move against the 4H trend · entry 15–60 min after the ORB

Fewer flags, stronger trades. That's why Flag Sizing halves size at 2+ flags instead of skipping the trade.
