// How it works

Four steps, then you trade like normal.

  1. 01
    Connect

    Sign in to Tradovate through its own consent screen. Add more logins if your accounts are spread across firms.

  2. 02
    Build a group

    Pick one leader account and the followers that copy it. Set each follower's size: a multiplier or a fixed quantity.

  3. 03
    Set your rules

    Loss limits, max contracts, trading hours, symbols, and news filters per account. Run the preflight check.

  4. 04
    Arm and trade

    Trade the leader however you normally do. Followers adjust to match. Start in dry-run mode to watch without sending orders.

It copies position, not individual orders. When the leader's position changes, each follower is moved to its target size. Partial fills, adds, scale-outs, and reversals land in the right place, and a repeated update can't double an order.

// What's inside
Copy groups

Several groups, each with its own leader, followers, hours, and filters. One leader can drive more than one group.

Per-account risk rules

Daily loss limit, daily profit lock, per-trade loss, max trades per day, and max contracts. Each account stops on its own.

Profit-target lock

Enter an account's size and target. When it reaches the target, it closes and stays locked until you unlock it.

Trading windows & filters

Copy only during the hours, symbols, and direction you choose. Outside them, followers can still exit but don't open.

News filter

Skip new entries around high-impact US releases, with optional flatten before the release.

Exits always copy

A paused, halted, or filtered follower still exits with the leader. Pausing never strands a position.

Preflight & dry run

A checklist before you arm: logins, stray positions, missing loss limits. Dry run logs what it would send.

One-click exit

Flatten one account, one group, or everything at once. A runaway-order breaker stops automatic orders on an account if something loops.

Trade log & alerts

Every copy, fill time, and slippage per account. Optional Discord alerts for halts and disconnects.

// A look inside
Read before you use it
  • It places real orders on your accounts. Test on demo or sim first.
  • Software can fail. Outages, disconnects, rejected orders, slippage, and missed or duplicate orders can happen.
  • Followers copy the leader's position, not its stop or target orders. If the copier stops, followers have no stop of their own.
  • Risk rules are checked on a timer and can be exceeded in fast markets.
  • Check your prop firm's rules. Some firms ban or restrict trade copiers.
  • Watch your accounts while it runs. You are responsible for every trade and every loss.

// Questions
Can I copy FSD-X's trades or another member's?

No. You can only copy between accounts on Tradovate logins you connect yourself.

Which brokers and platforms does it work with?

Tradovate accounts, including prop firm accounts that run on Tradovate. You can trade the leader from Tradovate, TradingView, or any platform that routes to that Tradovate account.

How many accounts can I copy to?

There are no per-account fees and no account tiers. Connect up to 10 Tradovate logins and copy to the accounts on them. Your prop firm's own account limits still apply.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. It runs on FSD-X servers. You still need to watch your accounts while it's armed.

Is it allowed by my prop firm?

It depends on the firm and the account type. Some allow copiers, some limit them, some ban them. Check your firm's current rules before you connect a funded or evaluation account.

What else comes with it?

The full FSD-X platform and Nexus 2.0: dashboard, trade journal, account tracker, backtest workspace, playbook, and trade importer. The indicator suite, Auto-Trader, Scout Alerts, and the live trade room are part of the FSD-X membership, not this plan.

What happens to my data?

Your Tradovate account data is used only to run the copier for you. Access tokens are encrypted. Remove a login at any time to cut access. Full details are in the Copy Trader Terms.
