// Two things people get wrong
Nightwing isn't a cheap Raven

They're built for different people. Nightwing gives you nothing to put on a chart — it's for a trader who already has a strategy and wants the record-keeping around it. Raven puts levels on the chart. If you want something to trade from, Nightwing isn't the cheaper option, it's the wrong one.

Raven isn't a cut-down Knightfall

Same range detection, same stop placement, same target selection — so the levels are identical. What Knightfall adds is judgment: it grades each breakout and refuses the ones that don't qualify. If you want that call made for you, pay for it. If you'd rather make it yourself, paying for it would only get in your way.
