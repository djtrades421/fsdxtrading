How it works

No contract to sign. No minimums. You can stop any time.

STEP 01

You apply

Form below. We read every one and reply either way, usually within a few days.

STEP 02

We agree a rate

Set individually based on your audience and how you plan to promote. Agreed in writing before anything goes live.

STEP 03

You get a link

Issued through Whop, tied to your account. It can point at this site, not just our Whop page.

STEP 04

Whop pays you

Commission clears after a 30-day holding period, then repeats each cycle the member renews. Your own dashboard shows clicks and conversions.

The trial comes first

Everyone you send starts on the free trial. Commission begins on their first real payment, not at signup — and it stops the month they cancel. Plan for a lag before anything lands.

Your starting rate isn't your rate forever

We'd rather pay a good partner properly than lose them.

30% is the baseline — the same rate our own members earn. If you're coming in with real reach or a specific plan, we'll talk about starting higher. And if it works — if you're steadily bringing in members who stick around — we revisit it upward from wherever you started. The top end of what we pay is 50%.

There's no tier chart and no threshold to hit, on purpose. Publishing a number just gets it chased, and volume isn't what we're after. We look at who's actually helping and we adjust. You can raise it with us any time — you won't be the first.
