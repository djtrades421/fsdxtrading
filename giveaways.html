Avoiding impersonators

We will never ask you to pay a fee, send crypto, or hand over broker or account credentials to claim a prize. Winners are contacted on the same channel the giveaway ran on, from the accounts listed above. Anyone doing otherwise is not us — report and block them.

The rules that always apply

Each giveaway carries its own full rules in its own post — prize, entry period, eligibility and how a winner is picked. The terms below apply to all of them.

No purchase necessary

No purchase, payment or membership is necessary to enter or win any FSD-X giveaway, and buying something does not improve your chance of winning.

Who can enter

Entrants must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where they live, whichever is greater. Void where prohibited or restricted by law. Some prizes cannot be awarded in every country; where that applies it is stated in the giveaway post. FSD-X staff and their immediate families are not eligible.

Who runs them

Every giveaway is run and administered by FSD-X Trading alone. They are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram, Meta, YouTube, Google, X, Discord, or any prop firm, broker or platform whose product may appear as a prize. Any questions go to FSD-X, not to those companies.

Winners and prizes

Winners are selected at random from valid entries and announced on the channel the giveaway ran on. Odds depend on the number of entries received. Prizes are awarded as described, have no cash value, and cannot be transferred or exchanged. If a winner cannot be reached or does not respond within the time stated in the post, another entrant may be drawn. Any taxes are the winner's responsibility.

About evaluation accounts

Where the prize is a prop firm evaluation, the prize is the evaluation itself. Passing it, keeping funding and any profit is down to the winner's own trading, under that firm's rules. Winning an evaluation is not funding and is not income. Futures trading involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for every investor.

Questions about a giveaway? Contact us — or read the full disclosures.
