// TradingView change · November 1, 2026

What TradingView’s change means for you

TradingView is changing its rules for private (invite-only) scripts. From November 1, 2026, access to a paid private script can only be sold through the TradingView Marketplace. It can’t be sold through Whop, sold by us directly, or bundled into a membership. This is TradingView’s decision, and it applies to every script seller on TradingView, not just us.

Source: TradingView’s announcement. We’ve applied to TradingView’s Marketplace program and are waiting on their answer. This page will be updated as soon as we have it.

// Questions

Already a member?

Do I lose TradingView access on November 1?

No. TradingView has said access paid for before November 1 continues for the period already purchased, including lifetime access.

What happens when my membership renews after October 31?

We can’t renew the TradingView part through Whop after that date. Your platform, Nexus and Discord access carry on as normal. We’ll post the steps for keeping TradingView access in the Discord #announcements channel and on this page before November 1.

I bought lifetime access. Am I covered?

Yes. Lifetime spots bought before November 1 keep their TradingView access for life, per TradingView’s announcement, and include the NinjaTrader version when it’s released.

Do I have to switch to NinjaTrader?

No. You can stay on TradingView. NinjaTrader is an option if you’d rather keep everything in one plan with us.

Will prices change?

TradingView’s Marketplace sets how script access is billed, so TradingView pricing isn’t final yet. It’ll be posted here before November 1.
