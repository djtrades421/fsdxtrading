Getting started

Nexus runs as a Chrome side panel. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, then click the extension icon in your toolbar to open the panel. It stays open alongside TradingView or any other page. The expand icon in the top-right switches to full-window mode, which is worth using for the stats and chart views.

Tools

Optimizer — SETUP | RESULTSFree

Automates testing TradingView strategy parameters. It scans through input combinations and tracks results in real time, so you are not sitting there nudging one number at a time.

1
Open your TradingView chart with your strategy applied.
2
Go to TOOLS › OPTIMIZER › SETUP and click Scan Inputs.
3
Set the range and step size for each parameter to test.
4
Click Run Optimizer. Results appear under RESULTS as the scan runs.
5
Sort by Net Profit, Win Rate, Profit Factor or Max Drawdown.
6
Click Apply on any result to push those settings straight to your chart.
Works with any TradingView strategy
It does not require FSD-X ORB PRO, and it is free. If you build your own scripts this is worth having on its own.
Position Size Calculator — POS CALCFree

Work out your exact position size from your risk parameters. Handles futures and forex, with optional partial exit targets.

FieldWhat it is
Account SizeYour total account balance.
Risk Amount ($)The dollar amount you are willing to risk.
Stop Loss (pts/pips)Distance from entry to your stop.
TP1 / TP2Optional profit targets with partial exit percentages.

Outputs: contracts or lots, actual dollar risk, P&L per leg, total P&L, and R:R ratio.

Supported: MNQ, NQ, ES, MES, CL, GC, RTY, M2K, ZB, ZN and more, plus all major forex pairs in standard, mini and micro lots.

Watchlist & News Calendar — MY LIST | NEWSPro

Add tickers to monitor at a glance — up to 10. Use SCAN to run AI sentiment across every ticker on the list at once.

The economic calendar

A live calendar powered by ForexFactory data, loaded automatically when you open the NEWS sub-tab.

Date
Today / Tomorrow / This Week / custom date picker.
Currency
All, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, NZD.
Timezone
Auto-detects yours. ET, CT, MT, PT, GMT, CET, JST, AEST, GST, SGT available.
Impact
High / Medium / Low, each toggled on or off.

Each row shows time, currency, impact dots, event, forecast and previous. The next upcoming event is highlighted in green. Click FF to open ForexFactory for live actual values once an event releases.

Red dots = high impact  ·  yellow = medium  ·  grey = low.

Analysis

Chart Analyzer — UPLOAD | ANALYSISPro

Upload a chart screenshot and get an AI technical breakdown — price action and trend direction, key support and resistance, liquidity zones and order blocks, and a directional bias of bullish, bearish or neutral.

You can upload a higher timeframe chart alongside your primary one for confluence. Nexus cross-references both and gives a combined read. Works with general technical analysis and with the ORB PRO indicator.

News Scanner — SCANNER | RESULTSPro

Enter any ticker and get AI sentiment based on real-time headlines. Returns bullish, bearish or neutral with a summary of what is driving it.

1
Go to ANALYSIS › NEWS.
2
Enter a ticker symbol — MNQ, AAPL, SPY.
3
Click Scan News.
4
Review the summary and the headlines with their sentiment tags.
Flow Reader — INPUT | READINGPro

Paste options flow data and get an AI read on smart-money positioning. Works with flow data from any service.

1
Go to ANALYSIS › FLOW.
2
Paste your options flow data into the input field.
3
Click Analyze Flow.
4
Review the read on direction, conviction and notable activity.
Eval Sim — EVAL SIMPro

Runs Monte Carlo simulations on your trade data to estimate your probability of passing a prop firm evaluation.

Where it gets your trades
Nexus Journal
Pulls your logged trades in automatically.
CSV upload
Any trade history CSV.
Screenshot upload
AI reads the trade data out of an image.
ORB PRO backtest CSV
With grade filtering, so you can simulate only your best setups.

Firm presets: Apex Trader Funding, TopStep, MyFundedFutures, E8 Markets, or fully custom parameters.

Outputs: pass rate by contract size, a simulation chart, AI insights and recommendations.

Strategy Analyzer — STRATEGYPro

A deep-dive on your trading patterns to find where your edge actually lives. Breaks down performance by day of week, long vs short, win and loss streaks, and a consistency score. ORB PRO mode adds win rate and P&L by grade. It generates three to four insights per run, and you can export the results to CSV.

Risk Planner — RISK PLANNERPro

Enter your account parameters and see your maximum daily loss and total drawdown in dollars, an aggressiveness rating for your current settings, a risk spectrum from aggressive to conservative sizing, and a DD Protection Ladder showing when to scale down and how much runway remains at each level.

Journal

Your live trade journal, inside the browser. Five sub-tabs: CALENDAR, LOG TRADE, HISTORY, STATS and ACCOUNTS.

Log TradePro

Record a trade in full. Two entry modes: BY PRICE enters entry, TP1, TP2 and stop prices and Nexus calculates the P&L; MANUAL $ takes the dollar P&L directly for each leg.

FieldWhat it is
DateTrade date.
Entry / Exit TimeSession start and end.
TickerMNQ, NQ, ES and so on.
Account TypeFunded, Evaluation or Personal.
# of AccountsHow many accounts this trade was taken on.
DirectionLong or short.
TimeframeChart timeframe used.
GradeYour quality self-assessment, A+ through F.
ContractsContracts per account.
TP1 / TP2Partial exit targets with contract allocation.
Stop LossStop price level.
Chart URLOptional TradingView screenshot link.
NotesFree text.
History & trade detailPro

Every logged trade in a sortable list. Filter by ALL / WINS / LOSSES / BE, sort by date or net P&L by clicking a column header, and export to CSV any time.

Click any row to open the full trade detail — a read-only view of every field, with an edit button (pencil) that reopens it in Log Trade, and a delete button that removes it after confirmation.

Stats & P&L cardPro

Full performance breakdown, filtered by period (Today / This Week / This Month / All Time / Custom) and by account type.

MetricWhat it shows
Total TradesTrades in the selected period.
Win RateWins ÷ (wins + losses). Breakeven trades are excluded from the denominator.
Net P&LTotal profit or loss for the period.
Profit FactorGross profit divided by gross loss.
Cumulative P&LRunning P&L over time.
Outcome BreakdownWin / loss / BE counts and percentages.
Runner StatsTP1 hit rate, runner usage and outcomes, average TP1 P&L.
Day of WeekP&L and win rate by trading day.
Time of DayP&L and win rate by entry hour.
Trade SummaryAverage win and loss, best and worst trade, average duration.

The CARD button generates a shareable P&L card for the current filter selection — opens in a new tab, ready to screenshot.

Calendar & AccountsPro

CALENDAR gives you the month laid out day by day. ACCOUNTS is where you set up the accounts your trades are tagged to, which is what makes the account-type filters and per-account breakdowns work.

5

Backtest

Backtest sessions inside the extension, with the same four views as the journal — CALENDAR, LOG TRADE, HISTORY and STATS — but kept completely separate from your live trades.

Why it is separate
Backtest and replay trades do not belong in your live journal. Mixing them makes every stat on your journal wrong and untangling it afterwards is a chore. Sessions created here also appear on the site’s Backtest page, so you can start one in the extension and analyse it on a full screen later.
6

Playbook

Your trading plan, synced with the Playbook on the site. Four sub-tabs.

RULES
Rules you hold yourself to, grouped by stage — before the bell, sizing and risk, when it goes wrong, when it goes right, end of day.
CHECKLIST
The pre-session items you tick off before the open. Resets automatically each session.
SETUPS
Named setups with their conditions, one per line.
GOALS
Weekly and monthly targets.
It syncs both ways
What you write here shows up in the Playbook on fsdxtrading.com and vice versa. Set your rules once, see them wherever you are working.
7

Daily limits

The AI features are metered per day. Limits are enforced server-side and reset at midnight UTC.

FeatureRuns per day
Chart Analyzer5
News Scanner5
Eval Sim5
Flow Reader10
Watchlist scan3

The watchlist itself holds up to 10 tickers.

Everything else — the journal, backtest, playbook, optimizer, position size calculator, risk planner and the economic calendar — is unmetered.

Tips & best practices

Journalling
Log trades immediately after closing
Details fade fast.
Always fill in Grade and Notes
The Strategy Analyzer needs them to produce anything meaningful.
Add your chart link to every trade
So you can review the setup from the trade detail view later.
Use Exit Time
So your stats show accurate session durations.
The calendar
Check it before every session
High-impact events can invalidate ORB setups. Knightfall has no news filter by design — this is your job.
Use the Tomorrow filter the night before
Plan around what is coming.
The next-event marker updates live
So you always know what is next.
Eval Sim
Run at least 30 trades
Below that the simulation is not telling you much.
Use the grade filter in ORB PRO mode
To simulate only your best setups.
Test multiple contract sizes
To find the pass rate versus risk sweet spot.
General
Keep Nexus pinned alongside your charts
It is a side panel — it is meant to sit there.
Daily AI limits reset at midnight UTC
Not at your local midnight.
Use the expand icon
Full-window mode is much better for stats and charts.
